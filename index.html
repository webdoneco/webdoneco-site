FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your project with us.

How long does a website project take?+

Most websites take 2–6 weeks depending on complexity. A landing page can go live in 5 business days, while a full e-commerce or custom app typically takes 4–8 weeks. You'll get a precise timeline in your quote.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?+

Absolutely — we work with clients in UAE, UK, USA, Switzerland, Australia, and more. We offer flexible meeting times across multiple time zones and communicate in English and Urdu.

What's included after launch?+

All projects include 60 days of post-launch support at no extra cost — bug fixes, minor updates, and technical assistance. After that, affordable monthly maintenance plans are available.

How much does a website cost?+

Pricing varies by scope. WordPress sites from £500 / PKR 180,000. Shopify stores from £800. Custom web apps from £2,500. All quotes are fixed-price — no hidden surprises, ever.

Will my website work on mobile?+

100% — mobile-first is our default. Every site is fully responsive and tested on iOS, Android, and all major browsers before launch.

Can you take over my existing website?+

Yes. We offer audits, redesigns, and ongoing management for existing sites. We can also migrate to a better platform — WordPress, Shopify, or custom — whenever needed.

What is VantaOne Engine™?+

VantaOne is our proprietary AI-powered SEO and lead generation engine, currently in development. It will automatically surface your business to the right customers. Join the waitlist for early access.

Do you offer standalone SEO services?+

Yes — SEO doesn't require a new website. We offer VantaLaunch, VantaScale, and VantaAuthority packages, plus monthly retainers. Contact us for a free SEO audit.
