What does WebDone Co. do?+
WebDone Co. builds websites, SEO systems, CRM workflows, POS software, and online tools for businesses that want a professional online presence and better lead generation.
Where is WebDone Co. based?+
WebDone Co. is based in Pakistan and serves clients in the UK, UAE, USA, Switzerland, and worldwide.

Questions about WordPress, Shopify, Wix, custom development, redesigns, and responsive websites.

Which website platforms do you build on?+
We build WordPress, Shopify, Wix, and fully custom websites depending on the business goal, budget, timeline, and required features.

Yes. We can redesign an existing website, improve the layout, update the content structure, make it mobile-friendly, and prepare it for better conversion.

SEO

Questions about audits, timelines, local SEO, ranking expectations, and monthly SEO work.

Do you offer a free SEO audit?+
Yes. We offer a free basic SEO audit to identify major issues. A full deep SEO audit is also available for $99 only.
How long does SEO take?+
SEO usually takes time. Basic improvements can appear earlier, but stronger ranking growth often takes 3 to 6 months depending on competition, website quality, and content.
Do you guarantee first-page rankings?+
No honest SEO agency can guarantee exact rankings. We focus on technical improvements, content quality, keyword strategy, and consistent work that improves your chances of ranking.
Can you do local SEO?+
Yes. We can help with local keyword targeting, service pages, Google Business Profile guidance, local landing pages, and basic local visibility improvements.

Pricing & Process

Questions about quotes, timelines, payment stages, support, and project management.

Do you offer fixed-price quotes?+
Yes. Most projects are quoted as fixed-price after scope is confirmed, so both sides understand the cost before work starts.
How long does a website project take?+
Small websites can often take 1 to 3 weeks. Larger websites, Shopify stores, custom systems, or content-heavy projects can take longer depending on scope.
Do you provide support after launch?+
Yes. Standard projects include free post-launch support, and longer support can be arranged for larger or ongoing projects.
Do I need to pay everything upfront?+
Payment terms depend on project size. Usually, work starts with an upfront deposit, followed by milestone or final payment before launch.
