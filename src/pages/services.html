FAQ

Common Questions

Straight answers — no fluff.

How much does a website cost?+

WordPress from $699, Shopify from $999, Wix from $499, custom web apps from $3,499, SaaS products from $4,999. All fixed-price — you see the full breakdown before we start, with no hidden fees ever.

Which platform should I choose?+

WordPress suits content-heavy businesses, Shopify suits e-commerce, Wix suits fast-launch brands, and custom development suits complex or unique needs. We recommend the right fit free in your consultation.

Do I need a new website to start SEO?+

No — SEO works on your existing website. We start with a free audit, identify quick wins, and build a roadmap. A new site can help but is not required to start ranking better on Google.

Can you handle multiple services together?+

Absolutely — website plus SEO plus CRM in one project, one team, one invoice. Bundle pricing applies, often saving 15–25% versus hiring separate vendors.

What is website maintenance?+

Monthly care to keep your site secure, fast, and updated. Without it, plugins go outdated, security vulnerabilities appear, and performance drops. Plans from $69/month — less than one hour of freelancer time.

What happens after launch?+

60 days of free post-launch support is included — bug fixes, tweaks, and questions at no charge. After that, our maintenance plans and direct WhatsApp keep everything running smoothly.
