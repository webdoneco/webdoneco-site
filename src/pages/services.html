FAQ

Services FAQ

Quick answers to the most common questions about our services.

Can you handle multiple services in one project?+

Absolutely — it's our specialty. A common project is a new Shopify store plus SEO plus a CRM integration, all delivered by one team with one point of contact, so nothing falls through the cracks between vendors.

Which platform should I choose for my business?+

It depends on your goals. Content-heavy businesses often suit WordPress, product-based businesses suit Shopify, and fast-launch brands suit Wix. If your needs are complex or unique, custom development gives full flexibility. We'll recommend the best fit for free during your consultation.

Do I need a new website to start SEO?+

No — SEO works on your existing website. We start with a free audit, identify quick wins, and build a roadmap from there. A new website can help, but it's not a requirement to start ranking better.

Can a CRM or POS integrate with my new website?+

Yes — this is one of our most requested combinations. We design the website, CRM, and POS to work together from day one, so leads, orders, and inventory all sync automatically without manual data entry.

How do you price multi-service projects?+

Every project gets one fixed-price quote covering all requested services, broken down by component so you can see exactly what you're paying for. There's often a bundle discount versus hiring separate vendors for each piece.

What if I'm not sure what I need yet?+

That's completely normal — most clients start that way. Book a free consultation and our team will walk through your business goals and recommend the right combination of services, with no pressure or obligation.
