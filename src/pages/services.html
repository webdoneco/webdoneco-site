FAQ

Services FAQ

Quick answers to the most common questions about our services.

Can you handle multiple services in one project?+

Absolutely — it's our specialty. A common project combines a new website with SEO and CRM integration, all delivered by one team with one point of contact. Bundle pricing applies, saving you versus hiring separate vendors.

Which platform should I choose for my website?+

WordPress suits content-heavy businesses, Shopify suits e-commerce, Wix suits fast-launch brands. Custom development gives full flexibility for complex or unique needs. We recommend the right fit for free during your consultation.

How much does a website cost?+

WordPress sites from $699, Shopify stores from $999, Wix sites from $499, custom web apps from $3,499, and SaaS products from $4,999. All are fixed-price — you see the full breakdown before we start, with no hidden fees ever.

Do I need a new website to start SEO?+

No — SEO works on your existing website. We start with a free audit, identify quick wins, and build a roadmap. A new site can help but is not required to start ranking better on Google.

What is website maintenance and do I need it?+

Website maintenance keeps your site secure, fast, and updated. Without it, plugins go outdated, security vulnerabilities appear, and performance degrades. Our plans start at $69/month and cover updates, backups, security scanning, and monthly health reports.

What if I'm not sure what I need yet?+

That's completely normal — most clients start that way. Book a free consultation and we'll walk through your business goals and recommend the right combination of services, with no pressure or obligation.
