FAQ

Before You Reach Out

Quick answers to what happens after you submit the form.

What happens after I submit the form?+

We review your request and reply by email or WhatsApp within 24 hours — usually faster. We may ask a few follow-up questions before scheduling a free consultation call to discuss scope and provide a fixed-price quote.

Is the consultation really free?+

Yes, completely free with no obligation. We use the call to understand your goals and provide an accurate quote — you're never charged just for asking questions.

Do I need to know exactly what I want before contacting you?+

Not at all. Many clients start with a rough idea — "I need a website" or "my current site isn't working." We'll help you figure out the right approach during the consultation.

What if my budget is smaller than your packages?+

Tell us anyway. We'll be upfront about what's realistic for your budget and suggest a scope that fits, or a phased approach to get you started within your means.

Can you help me choose the right platform?+

Yes. If you are unsure between WordPress, Shopify, Wix, custom development, SEO, CRM, or POS, choose “Not Sure Yet” in the form and we will recommend the most practical option for your goals and budget.

Can I contact you on WhatsApp instead of filling the form?+

Yes. WhatsApp is perfect for quick questions, screenshots, and early project discussions. The detailed form simply helps us give a more accurate quote faster.

Do you work with international clients?+

Yes. WebDone Co. works with clients in Pakistan, the UK, UAE, USA, Switzerland, and worldwide. We can communicate by email, WhatsApp, or online call depending on your preference.
