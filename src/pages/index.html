FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your project with us.

How long does a website project take?+

Timelines depend on scope, content readiness and integrations. We provide a delivery estimate after reviewing your requirements.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?+

Absolutely — our web development agency works with clients in UAE, UK, USA, Switzerland, Australia, Pakistan, India and more. We offer flexible meeting times across time zones and communicate fluently in English and Urdu. We collaborate remotely with businesses across time zones.

What's included after launch?+

We can include launch assistance and ongoing maintenance based on your project agreement. Support coverage is documented in the proposal.

How much does a website cost?+

Pricing depends on scope, integrations and support needs. We provide a clear project estimate after discussing your requirements.

Will my website work on mobile?+

100% — mobile-first is our default. Every site is fully responsive and tested on iOS, Android, and all major browsers before launch.

Can you take over my existing website?+

Yes. We offer audits, redesigns, and ongoing management for existing sites. We can also migrate to a better platform — WordPress, Shopify, or custom — whenever needed.

Do you offer standalone SEO services?+

Yes — SEO doesn't require a new website. We provide technical, on-page and local SEO support. Contact us to discuss an SEO audit and a scope suited to your goals.
