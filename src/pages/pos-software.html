Can you build POS for retail and restaurants?+
Yes. We can build POS workflows for retail shops, service counters, small restaurants, salons, warehouses, and multi-branch operations.
Can the POS manage inventory?+
Yes. Inventory can include products, categories, variants, purchases, suppliers, stock alerts, returns, and branch stock.
Can we use it for multiple branches?+
Yes. Multi-branch POS can show branch-wise sales, stock, staff, and central owner reports.
How long does a POS system take?+
A focused POS can take 3–5 weeks. Multi-branch or advanced inventory systems can take 6–10+ weeks depending on scope.
Can staff have different login access?+
Yes. We can create cashier, manager, owner, inventory, and admin roles with different permissions.
Can reports be exported?+
Yes. Reports can be designed for exports depending on your requirements, such as sales summaries, product movement, or branch reports.
