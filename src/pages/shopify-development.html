FAQ

Shopify FAQ

Answers to the common questions clients ask before starting a Shopify project.

Is Shopify better than WordPress for online stores?

If your main goal is selling products, Shopify is usually easier to manage and more reliable for checkout. WordPress is better for content-heavy or complex non-store websites.

Can you upload products for us?

Yes. Product setup can be included based on your package and product count. We can also clean titles, organize collections, and structure variants.

Do you set up payment and shipping?

Yes. We configure the key Shopify settings, payment methods, shipping zones, policies, taxes basics, and checkout settings where available.

Can you redesign an existing Shopify store?

Yes. We can improve the layout, mobile experience, homepage structure, product pages, navigation, conversion sections, and visual quality.

Will my Shopify store be SEO-ready?

We set the basic SEO foundation including page titles, meta descriptions, headings, image alt text guidance, clean URLs, and collection structure.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Every project includes post-launch support so small fixes, launch questions, and early adjustments can be handled smoothly.
