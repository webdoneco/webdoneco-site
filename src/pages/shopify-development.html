FAQ

Shopify questions, answered

Is Shopify better than WordPress for e-commerce?

If your main goal is selling products online, Shopify is usually simpler and more reliable. WordPress is better when content, custom publishing, or highly flexible website structures are more important.

Can you redesign my existing Shopify store?

Yes. We can improve homepage sections, product pages, collection pages, navigation, mobile layout, and conversion elements without rebuilding everything from scratch.

Do you add products too?

Yes, depending on the package. We can upload a starter set of products and guide you on how to manage the rest after launch.

Can you connect email marketing and tracking?

Yes. We can connect tools such as email marketing, analytics, Meta/TikTok pixels, reviews, WhatsApp, and other Shopify apps based on your business needs.

Will my Shopify store be SEO-friendly?

Yes. We set up basic technical and on-page SEO foundations, including page titles, meta descriptions, URL structure, alt text guidance, and redirects where needed.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Every project includes post-launch support, and we can also provide ongoing updates, CRO improvements, or Shopify maintenance if needed.
