FAQ

Shopify Questions, Answered

Is Shopify better than WordPress for e-commerce?

For product-first businesses, usually yes. Shopify gives you a strong checkout, product management, payment setup, and e-commerce tools from day one.

Can you redesign my existing Shopify store?

Yes. We can refresh the design, improve page structure, speed, product pages, cart flow, and app setup without starting from zero.

Do you upload products?

Yes. Depending on the package, we can upload products, organize collections, set variants, optimize titles, and prepare SEO basics.

Will I be able to manage it myself?

Yes. Shopify is easy to manage, and we provide handover guidance so you can update products, orders, discounts, and content confidently.
