FAQ

Shopify Questions, Answered

Quick answers before we discuss your store in detail.

How much should a Shopify store cost?+
For Option B pricing, I suggest $699 for a Launch Store, $1,299 for a Growth Store, and $2,499+ for a Scale Store. It is competitive but still premium enough for international clients.
Will the store look like a template?+
No. Even when a premium theme is used for speed and budget, the layout, sections, visuals, spacing, content structure, and brand feel are customized.
Can you set up payments and shipping?+
Yes. We can configure payment gateways, shipping rules, tax basics, policy links, checkout settings, and basic launch testing.
Can you upload products?+
Yes. Product upload can be included depending on package and catalog size. For large catalogs, we usually import products from a prepared CSV.
Do you handle Shopify SEO?+
We include basic Shopify SEO structure such as titles, meta descriptions, clean collections, alt text guidance, and sitemap/indexing readiness.
Can you migrate from another platform?+
Yes. We can help migrate from WooCommerce, Wix, custom websites, or another Shopify store. Migration scope depends on products, customers, orders, and content.
Do you provide support after launch?+
Yes. Shopify packages include post-launch support so small fixes, guidance, and launch issues can be handled after the store goes live.
Can you improve an existing Shopify store?+
Yes. We can redesign key sections, improve product pages, clean up apps, improve speed basics, and fix conversion issues without rebuilding everything.
