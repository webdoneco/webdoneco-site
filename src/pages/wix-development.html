FAQ

Wix Questions, Answered.

Clear answers before you start your Wix website project.

Is Wix good for business websites?

Yes. Wix is a good choice for service businesses, local brands, consultants, and small companies that need a professional website they can update without a developer.

Do you build in Wix Studio?

Yes. Wix Studio is useful when a project needs more design control, advanced responsive behavior, or a more custom-feeling layout.

Can you redesign my existing Wix website?

Yes. We can rebuild the layout, improve content structure, make the mobile design cleaner, and fix sections that look outdated or cluttered.

Will the website be SEO-ready?

We handle basic SEO foundations including page titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, clean page flow, image guidance, and Google setup recommendations.

Can I manage the site myself after launch?

Yes. That is one of the biggest reasons to choose Wix. We keep the structure clean so basic text, images, forms, and sections are easier to update.

When should I choose WordPress instead?

Choose WordPress when you need heavier blogging, custom plugins, advanced membership/LMS, or a larger content system. We can help you compare both before starting.
