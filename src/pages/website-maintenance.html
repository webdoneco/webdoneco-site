Yes. We first review the website's platform, hosting, access and existing issues before confirming a maintenance scope.

Is maintenance a monthly service?

Ongoing support can be arranged on a recurring basis or by an agreed project scope. Pricing depends on the website and support needs.

No provider can reasonably guarantee zero downtime. We focus on prevention, monitoring options and practical incident response.
