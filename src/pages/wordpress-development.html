FAQ

WordPress FAQ

Deep-dive answers to the questions we hear most about WordPress development.

How much does a WordPress website cost?+

WordPress projects start from £500 for a brochure site and scale up depending on complexity, custom functionality, and WooCommerce requirements. Every quote is fixed-price, broken down by component, and agreed before any work begins.

Do you build custom themes or use pre-made ones?+

We build fully custom themes from scratch for most projects, ensuring unique design and optimal performance — no bloated page-builder code. For tighter budgets or timelines, we can also heavily customise premium themes.

Will I be able to update content myself after launch?+

Yes — that's the whole point of WordPress. We build with an intuitive admin experience and provide a short training walkthrough at launch so you (or your team) can confidently add pages, posts, and products without touching code.

Is WordPress secure enough for my business?+

Yes, when built and maintained properly. We harden every install with security plugins like Wordfence, disable unnecessary file editing, enforce strong login policies, and set up automated backups — the same standards used on enterprise WordPress sites.

Can you migrate my existing WordPress or other CMS site?+

Yes. We handle migrations from outdated WordPress installs, Wix, Squarespace, and other platforms — preserving your content, SEO rankings, and traffic with proper URL redirects and a structured migration plan.

Do I need to pay for plugins and hosting separately?+

Most plugins we use have free tiers sufficient for typical projects; premium plugin licenses (if needed) are itemised clearly in your quote. Hosting is separate from our development fee — we can recommend reliable providers or work with your existing host.

What's the difference between your Starter, Business, and Enterprise packages?+

Starter suits simple brochure sites with up to 5 pages. Business adds custom theme design, WooCommerce, and advanced SEO for growing companies. Enterprise covers complex builds — membership sites, custom plugins, and large-scale stores with priority support.

How fast will my WordPress site load?+

We target 90+ Google PageSpeed scores on every build through optimised images, efficient caching (WP Rocket), clean code, and CDN delivery via Cloudflare. Our average across delivered WordPress sites is 98/100.
