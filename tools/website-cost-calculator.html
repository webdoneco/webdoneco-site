FAQ

Website Cost Questions Answered

How much does a website cost in 2025?+

Costs range from $499 for a simple Wix site to $20,000+ for a complex custom web app. The average small business website costs $700–$2,500. At WebDone Co. all prices are fixed — use this calculator for an instant estimate.

Is the price really fixed — no hidden costs?+

Yes. Every project at WebDone Co. is quoted at a fixed price agreed before work begins. The only exception is changes to scope that you request after work starts — which we always price transparently before proceeding.

Does the price include hosting and domain?+

Development price does not include hosting or domain registration as these vary by provider. We recommend the right hosting for your project and help with setup. Typical hosting costs $10–$50 per month.

How long does it take to build a website?+

A simple 5-page WordPress site takes 2–3 weeks. A custom e-commerce store typically takes 4–6 weeks. A complex SaaS MVP takes 8–16 weeks. You receive an exact timeline in your free quote.

Do you offer payment plans?+

Yes. Most projects are split into milestone payments: typically 50% upfront and 50% on completion. For larger projects we can arrange three-stage payments. We accept bank transfer, PayPal, and major cards.
